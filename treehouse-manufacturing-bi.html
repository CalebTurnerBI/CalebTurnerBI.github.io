Graduate Business Intelligence Project · Business Case

Manufacturing Performance and Operational Intelligence

A proposed BI initiative to consolidate manufacturing performance information and give operations leaders a more consistent view of production, downtime, quality, and improvement opportunities.

Project Overview

This project develops a business case for a manufacturing operations intelligence initiative at TreeHouse Foods. The proposal addresses a common operational challenge: production, quality, maintenance, and enterprise data can be spread across separate systems, making it harder to create a consistent view of performance.

The proposed solution uses Microsoft Fabric and Power BI to establish a scalable data foundation and support reliable KPI reporting. The work is an academic proposal and planning exercise; it does not represent a system deployed at TreeHouse Foods or verified operational results.

Business Problem & Objectives

The business case focuses on improving visibility across operational performance by bringing relevant data together, standardizing KPI definitions, and making information easier to analyze.

  • Reduce fragmented reporting across production, quality, maintenance, and related operational sources.
  • Establish consistent definitions for manufacturing performance measures.
  • Give decision-makers a clearer view of output, downtime, quality, and operational trends.
  • Create a foundation that can expand as data quality, integration, and user adoption mature.

Proposed KPI Focus

Production Performance

Production output and Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE).

Equipment Reliability

Downtime patterns and equipment-related production interruptions.

Quality

Defect rate and quality performance trends.

Operational Efficiency

Changeover duration and labor efficiency as the solution matures.

Implementation Approach

The proposal recommends an initial six-month pilot with a controlled scope, followed by evaluation and phased expansion.

  • Foundation: confirm business requirements, source-system availability, KPI definitions, data ownership, and data quality needs.
  • Integration and reporting: build the initial data pipelines and analytical model, then develop and validate priority Power BI reporting.
  • Evaluation and expansion: assess data reliability, user adoption, and business value before expanding coverage and adding further measures.
